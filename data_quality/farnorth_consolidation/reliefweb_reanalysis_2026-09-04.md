# Far North ReliefWeb re-analysis (2015–2025)

The ReliefWeb directory currently contains 104 PDFs. The existing inventory has
opened/text-extracted all 104; 162 Far North flood-related pages were retained
for evidence review. The five XLSX files were also read sheet-by-sheet and are
audited in `reliefweb_xlsx_file_audit.csv`.

## Solidarités International passage

The supplied French text is the Solidarités International article **“Extrême-
Nord du Cameroun : une réponse d’urgence déployée face aux inondations”**,
published **18 November 2019**:

<https://www.solidarites.org/fr/pays/cameroun/une-reponse-durgence-deployee-face-aux-inondations-a-lextreme-nord-du-cameroun/>

It establishes a real 2019 flood impact and names the **Logone-et-Chari**
department (more than 20,000 people affected there), but describes “recent
months” rather than a bounded onset/end window. It is therefore **supporting
evidence for the existing 2019 Far North episode**, not a new independent
episode. The publication date must not be substituted for the flood date.

## 2015 question

No reviewed source establishes a dated and geographically bounded 2015 flood
episode. The 2015 references found are aggregate displacement statistics or
general flood-season statements. Under the project rule, they remain
`GENERAL_FLOOD_REFERENCE` and are not added as positive event labels.

## Result

No additional independent episode was promoted in this re-analysis. The
canonical Far North total remains **13 independent verified episodes**. The
full source-level XLSX decisions are in
`data_quality/farnorth_consolidation/reliefweb_xlsx_file_audit.csv`.
